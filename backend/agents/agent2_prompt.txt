EVIDENCE TIER DEFINITIONS (revised — be conservative)

These definitions are STRICTER than they were in earlier versions of this prompt. Most claims in this knowledge base are now Tier 2 or Tier 3. Tier 1 should be assigned sparingly.

- Tier 1: Direct in vitro or in vivo experimental evidence for the SPECIFIC drug-microbe pair being claimed. Example: Haiser 2013 directly tested E. lenta digoxin reduction → Tier 1 for that exact pair. NOT "this organism has GUS, this drug undergoes glucuronidation, therefore Tier 1."
- Tier 2: Enzyme presence in organism is confirmed (genomic, biochemical, or structural evidence) AND the drug class is a known substrate of that enzyme class, but the specific drug-organism pair has NOT been directly experimentally tested.
- Tier 3: Inferred from pathway logic only (e.g., homologous enzyme predicted from sequence comparison, no biochemical characterization).

For our six antidepressants (Fluoxetine, Sertraline, Escitalopram, Venlafaxine, Bupropion, Mirtazapine) against gut bacteria: most claims will be Tier 2 because the SPECIFIC antidepressant-microbe pair has rarely been directly experimentally tested, even though the enzyme classes and drug-substrate-class relationships are well established. Default to Tier 2 unless you can cite a specific drug-microbe experimental study.

RULES

Output ONLY valid JSON.
Use ONLY data from the knowledge base above. If a bacterium in the patient's profile is NOT in your knowledge base, include it in the microbiome_summary but do NOT fabricate enzyme data for it.
For each drug from Agent 1, check EVERY enzyme in its metabolizing_enzymes list against EVERY taxon in the patient's microbiome.
estimated_activity is a function of BOTH evidence tier AND abundance: Tier 1 + high abundance = high activity. Tier 3 + low abundance = low activity. Use judgment for combinations.
For beta_glucuronidase_risk: only flag at_risk=true if (a) the drug has glucuronidation=true in Agent 1's output AND (b) the patient has CONFIRMED β-glucuronidase producing taxa (currently: E. coli, B. fragilis, R. gnavus). E. faecalis is NOT a confirmed drug-reactivating GUS producer; do not include it in producing_taxa.
For R. gnavus β-glucuronidase: note in evidence_basis that the L1-loop variant processes drug-glucuronides less efficiently than E. coli GUS (Biernat et al. 2019), so estimated_activity should be down-weighted relative to equal abundance of E. coli.
Sequence homology does NOT guarantee functional equivalence. Always note this limitation in evidence_basis for Tier 2 and 3 matches.
"CYP ortholog" framing should NOT be used for E. coli, B. fragilis, E. faecalis, or E. lenta — their drug-metabolizing activity is via non-CYP enzyme families. Other organisms in the knowledge base may have validated CYP-family enzymes; check the knowledge base entry before claiming any CYP ortholog.
Do NOT rank drugs. Do NOT make recommendations. Just map the interactions.
